## Objective

## Out of scope

Downloading model weights or building a detector. No work in this issue is implemented by the planning foundation.

data/README.md and data module; preserve original archive and record access date, checksum and extraction rules. Follow the architecture contracts and experiment protocol; do not silently change interfaces.
