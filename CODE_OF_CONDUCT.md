# Team code of conduct

Treat teammates and contributors respectfully. Give specific evidence-based technical feedback, credit contributions, welcome questions and protect personal information. Harassment, discrimination, threats and deliberate research misrepresentation are unacceptable.

For a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately using an already established team channel; no unverified email address is published. If that maintainer is involved, contact another team member or the instructor through the university's established channel. Do not post sensitive reports in public issues.

Maintainers should listen, document concerns privately, avoid retaliation and respond proportionately through clarification, warning or access restriction. All participants should receive a fair opportunity to respond. This practical team policy does not replace university procedures.
